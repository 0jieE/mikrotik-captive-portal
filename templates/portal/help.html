The idea in one minute

The school Wi-Fi is open to join, but the internet stays locked until you sign in with your own username and password on this portal.

  1. You join the Wi-Fi.
  2. The sign-in page opens. You type your username and password.
  3. The portal checks your account, your semester and your data left.
  4. It tells the Wi-Fi router to let your device online, at your plan's speed.

You sign in only once. You never type anything into the router itself - the portal does that for you.

1. Create your account (first time only)

  1. Connect to the school Wi-Fi. When the sign-in page appears, tap Create an account.
  2. Fill in your name, choose a username (no spaces) and a password, then tap Register.
  3. Wait for the ICT office to approve your registration. Until then, signing in will say "waiting for approval".
  4. Once approved, sign in with the same username and password.

Your username is not case-sensitive when you sign in - JDelaCruz and jdelacruz work the same.

2. Sign in and get online

  1. Join the school Wi-Fi. A sign-in window opens by itself. If it does not, open a browser and go to any website - you will be sent to the sign-in page.
  2. Type your username and password and tap Connect.
  3. That's it - you are online.

The sign-in window closes by itself. As soon as your phone sees that the internet works, it closes that small window. This is normal - you are still connected.

3. Your dashboard (check your data any time)

Open this address in your normal browser while on the school Wi-Fi:

{{ portal_url }}

Sign in with your username and password. Because the sign-in window was closed, the browser asks you once more - this does not disconnect you; it shows "You are already connected" and opens your dashboard. Bookmark the page or use Add to Home screen.

  • Home - connected or not, your device's address, how long you've been online, data used this semester and your plan's speed.
  • History - your past sessions: when, how long, how much data, and why each one ended.
  • Password - change your password.
  • Disconnect - takes your device off the internet but keeps you on the dashboard.
  • Log out - signs you out and disconnects your device.

Data, speed and devices

Your plan
Every account has a plan set by the ICT office: download and upload speed, and usually a data allowance for the semester. Your dashboard shows it.
Data allowance
Data used is counted for the whole semester. When it runs out you are disconnected and cannot get back online until the ICT office changes your plan. You can still sign in to see your dashboard.
One device at a time
Your account works on one device at a time. Signing in on a second device disconnects the first one.
Time limits
Some plans end a session after a set time, and the router may disconnect a device that has been idle or has left the Wi-Fi. Just sign in again.
Semesters
Internet access is open only while a semester is active, and your account belongs to that semester. At the start of a new semester the ICT office opens your account again - if it says you have no account for this semester, ask them.

Password

  • Change it under Password on your dashboard.
  • Forgot it? Ask the ICT office. They give you a temporary password, and you must choose a new one the first time you sign in with it.
  • After 5 wrong tries the sign-in is locked for 5 minutes. Wait, then try again.
  • Never share your password - everything done on your account counts against your data.
