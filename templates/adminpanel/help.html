How it works

Three parts work together:

The portal (this app)
Holds the instructors, semesters, plans and usage. It decides who may go online. This is where you make every change.
The MikroTik router
Runs the Wi-Fi hotspot. It blocks the internet for every device until the portal tells it to let one in, and it enforces the speed limit and data allowance.
The background sync
Every 30 seconds it reads usage from the router, records it in the portal, closes sessions that ended, and disconnects anyone whose allowance is used up or whose access was removed.

What happens when an instructor signs in

  1. The phone joins the Wi-Fi; the router sends it to the portal sign-in page.
  2. The portal checks the password, approval, active semester, account and data left.
  3. It updates the instructor's hotspot user on the router and logs that device in.
  4. A session is recorded and shows in Live monitoring.
  • Instructors only ever type their portal username and password. The router passwords are random and never shown.
  • One device per instructor: a new sign-in moves the connection to the new device.
  • If the instructor is already online and opens the portal again, they go straight to their dashboard.

Start of a semester (do these in order)

  1. Bandwidth policies - Bandwidth policies. Each plan has download/upload speed, an optional data quota for the semester, and an optional session time limit. Mark one as default - it is used when you approve someone without choosing a plan. Saving a plan sends it to the router.
  2. Semester - Semesters. Add the school year and the semester, then Activate it. Only one semester is active at a time, and the internet is closed when none is.
  3. Give instructors an account for this semester - see the next section.

Instructors

An instructor can go online only when all of these are true: the user is active, the registration is approved, a semester is active, they have an account (with a plan) for that semester, the account is enabled, and the data quota is not used up.

They register themselves
New sign-ups appear with a badge on Instructors. Click Approve (default plan) or open the instructor to choose a plan. Approving also creates their account for the active semester. Reject blocks the registration.
You add one
Add instructor on the Instructors page. A temporary password is shown once; the instructor must change it at first sign-in.
You import a list
Upload masterlist: download the template (username, first, middle, last name, extension), fill it in, upload, check the preview, confirm. Print the credentials sheet with the temporary passwords - it is shown only once.
Bulk actions
Tick instructors, then Bulk action: approve, reject, enable, disable, or change bandwidth policy. Changing the policy also creates the account for the active semester if it is missing.
One instructor's page
See usage and sessions, change the plan, enable/disable, reset password (temporary password shown once), or push to router to resend their settings.

Daily work

Dashboard
Number of instructors, who is online, active accounts, router status, data used over the last 7 days, and recent activity.
Live monitoring
Every connected device with its address, time online and data. Kick disconnects a device; the instructor can sign in again if still allowed (disable the account to keep them off).
Reports
Usage by day and by instructor for a date range. Export to CSV or PDF.
System logs
Every sign-in (successful or refused, with the reason), approval, change and kick, with who did it and when. Look here first when an instructor reports a problem.

Changing to a new semester

Activating a new semester closes the old one: every account of the old semester is turned off on the router and everyone online is disconnected. The confirmation page shows how many will be affected.

Accounts do not carry over. After activating, open Instructors, select everyone who should keep access, and use Bulk action → Change bandwidth policy to give them an account in the new semester. Usage starts again from zero.

Admin and superuser accounts (superusers only)

  • On Staff accounts, add the people who run the portal and choose their role: Admin (portal admin) or Superuser.
  • You cannot deactivate yourself, the last active account, or the last active superuser, and you cannot remove your own superuser role.
  • Everyone changes their own password from the menu at the top right.

Problems and fixes

Instructor saysCheck / fix
"Wrong username or password"Find them on Instructors (search the username). Reset the password if needed.
"Waiting for approval"Approve them on Instructors.
"No internet account for the current semester"Open the instructor and assign a plan (creates the account).
"No active semester"Activate a semester on Semesters.
"Data quota is used up"Move them to a plan with a bigger quota, or one without a quota.
"Account disabled"Enable the account on the instructor's page.
"Could not detect your device"Ask them to turn Wi-Fi off and on and sign in from the page that opens by itself.
"Network controller is not responding"The portal cannot reach the router. Check the router is on and connected, then look at the dashboard's router status.
"Too many failed attempts"They must wait 5 minutes (5 wrong passwords in a row).
Online, but the speed or limit looks wrongOn Bandwidth policies use Sync, or Push to router on the instructor's page.
